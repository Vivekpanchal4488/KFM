Operational Service Standards

By engaging Kesari Facility Management (KFM) for integrated facility services, manned security guarding, soft services, technical operations, or STP/WTP maintenance, clients agree to the following terms and operational standards.

1. Scope of Facility Services

KFM provides specialized facility management services under agreed Service Level Agreements (SLAs), including:

  • Soft Services: Housekeeping, daily cleaning, pest control, landscape & gardening, and swimming pool maintenance.
  • Security & Vigilance: Manned security guarding, police-verified personnel, access control, and 24x7 night vigilance patrols.
  • Hard Technical & Water Operations: Electrical services, plumbing services, STP maintenance, WTP maintenance, and CCTV operation.

2. Statutory Compliance & Labor Law Adherence

KFM operates with 100% adherence to Government Statutory Rules and Labour Laws in India:

  • All personnel are enrolled under Provident Fund (PF) and Employees' State Insurance (ESI) schemes.
  • Manpower wages strictly conform to the Minimum Wages Act as applicable in Karnataka.
  • Monthly statutory payment challans (PF/ESI) are submitted to client associations upon request.

Clients (Residential Apartment Associations, Commercial Complexes, and Builders) shall provide safe working access, necessary utility connections (water and electricity for scrubbers/pumps), and designated staff room facilities required for smooth daily operations.
